Architecture Overview

  • Core relational model with foreign keys and read-only views (pd.v_*)
  • ETL normalizes inputs, stores aliases, and queues uncertain cases
  • Safe SQL layer restricts dynamic queries (SELECT-only + enforced LIMIT)
  • Embeddings table supports AI search and recommendations
  • Data quality checks: nullability contract, FK orphan detection, duplicate keys

SQL Questions & Results

Q1 — Spring 2026 Completed Student Alumni

Q2 — Hofstra Applicants in 2024

Applicants with primary school = Hofstra University, within calendar year 2024.

Q3 — Corporate Partners Sponsoring Fall 2025

Distinct sponsor count, program-sponsor detail, and sponsor account type verification.

Q4 — Spring 2026 Alumni in Real Estate Development

Most recent jobs mapped; count and student→firm detail where industry_primary = Real Estate Development.

Q5 — Next Corporate Partner to Re‑Engage

Ranking by most stale program term + program counts; outcome rollup (completions vs participants) to justify re‑engagement.
